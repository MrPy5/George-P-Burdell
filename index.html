The Most Famous Person who Never Existed

George P. Burdell is one of Georgia Tech’s most famous alumni, yet no one has seen his face. In 1927, William Edgar Smith received 2 application letters to Georgia Tech. He decided to submit the extra one and used the name George P. Burdell, after his high school principal George P. Butler and his friend's mothers maiden name. Butler was a huge UGA fan and was even the captain of their football team back in 1893. Both applications were accepted, but William didn’t stop there. He enrolled Burdell in the same classes as himself and did homework and tests for him with the help of friends. In 1930, they both graduated and received degrees from Georgia Tech.
